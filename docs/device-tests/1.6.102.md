# Testing 1.6.102 on phones

Date: 2026-10-30. Tested by: Claude Code over adb (installing the build, debug console, `modkit.log` of both phones), without a player.

| Phone | Android | Resolution | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| Xiaomi 25042PN24C | 16 | 1440×3200 | host |
| Google Pixel 3a | 12 | 1080×2220 | guest |

Connection: `call <session>.Host` at the host and `.Join` at the guest through `coop_debug`.

Version on the phones: `кооператив 1.6.102 включён`: host at 20:08:37, guest at 20:09:02.

Battle mode: Terra zone, shared enemies on (`world=1`, `sendHz=30`), shared pause on. Both phones had `coop_debug` and `coop_cheat` enabled (levels go up every few seconds, so shared skill choice pauses are very frequent). Heroes immortal: `skip C_Battle_AllyUnit Unit_Death 0`. Skills were picked automatically (the first card).

What changed since 1.6.98: the pauses for the player ahead on the clock ("start", "clock", "to the boss") are removed; the lagging player catches up with a ×1.25 / ×1.5 / ×2 speed-up (from 2 / 4 / 8 s behind), and in the 30 s before a boss wave from 1 s and one step higher. The speed-up goes through the static `C_Battle_Manager.TimeScaleBonus`, corrected for the game's frame-rate slowdown, at most ×4.

Why (analysis of `libil2cpp.so` and the console, 30.10): every frame `C_Battle_Manager.Update` sets `ApplyTimeScale = Time.timeScale = k × TimeScaleBonus`, where k = 0.1 × a rounded number derived from the frame rate, from 0.01 to 1. The mod's write to `Time.timeScale` was overwritten in the same frame (on 1.6.99: the guest at "×2" by `timeScale`, yet a 10 s gap didn't change over 30 s). The host has k = 1 (speed 1.18), the guest on the Pixel 3a has k = 0.5–0.8, so its clock fell 2 s behind again and again, even without a shift. The battle clock is `Play_Time`: `Game_SurvivalTime += 1` after each `WaitForSeconds(1)`.

## Known limitations

- Tested without a player, on two phones; the cheat makes shared pauses very frequent, so catching up is faster in a normal game.
- A simultaneous boss wave start at both isn't tested (see above): it needs a real battle up to 3:00.
- On 1.6.101, without smoothing k, the guest's speed while catching up jumped between 0.2 and 4.7; in 1.6.102 k is averaged.
- `am force-stop` while `coop` code is running is treated by ModKit as a crash, and it turns the mod off (the «Моды изменились» (Mods changed) window; «Как раньше» (As before) restores it).
