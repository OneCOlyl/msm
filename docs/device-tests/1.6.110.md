# Testing 1.6.110 on phones

Date: 2026-10-31. The user played; Claude Code installed the build and read the `modkit.log` logs of both phones over adb.

| Phone | Android | Resolution | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| Xiaomi 25042PN24C | 16 | 1440×3200 | host |
| Google Pixel 3a | 12 | 1080×2220 | guest |

Versions on the phones: `кооператив 1.6.110 включён` (host 01:43:04, guest 01:43:38).

Battle mode: Terra zone, shared enemies on, shared pause on.

What changed since 1.6.108: after the host picks up a normal obelisk, the mod sets `Battle_ClearON = 1` at the guest and restarts the `EnemyLevelUp` coroutine.

How it works in the game: `EnemyLevelUp` raises `EnemyLevelNum` and at a threshold from a table creates the merchant (`CreateItem_Character(12)` → `Play_MerchantTime` → `Create_Merchant`). During a boss wave the coroutine stops (`Create_UnitEnemy_StartLoop`), and after the boss `Play_AreaChange` starts it again. At the guest `Play_BossWaveEffect_End` doesn't run, the mod changes the area, and the coroutine stayed stopped.

## Logs

- Before the fix (1.6.109, 31.10): the guest's `EnemyLevelNum` after the first boss stayed at 150, while the host's reached 279; `MerchantNum` was 0 at the guest and 1 at the host. A manual `StartCoroutine EnemyLevelUp` at the guest through `coop_debug` moved the counter (153 → 173). `Battle_ClearON = 1` alone (1.6.109) didn't bring the merchant.
- No `ОШИБКА [coop]` or `обелиск: EnemyLevelUp:` lines.

## Known limitations

- The host's and guest's `EnemyLevelNum` weren't compared directly; the match is visible only from the merchant appearing.
- The "30 s before the boss" window in clock catch-up assumes a boss every 3 minutes; on Terra it's every 2:30.
