# Testing 1.6.116 on phones

Date: 2026-11-01. Tested by: Claude Code over adb (installing builds, debug console, `modkit.log` of both phones); the user watched the screens.

| Phone | Android | Resolution | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| Xiaomi 25042PN24C | 16 | 1440×3200 | host |
| Google Pixel 3a | 12 | 1080×2220 | guest |

Connection: `call <session>.Host` at the host and `.Join` at the guest through `coop_debug`.

Versions on the phones: `кооператив 1.6.116 включён` (host 19:19:17, guest 19:19:43). The 5:00 bosses were tested on 1.6.115 (same copy creation code).

Battle mode: Terra zone, shared enemies on (`world=1`, `sendHz=30`), shared pause on. `coop_debug` and `coop_cheat` on (all magic at max). Heroes: `skip C_Battle_AllyUnit Unit_Death 0` and `set hero.Unit_Hp 10000000` every 0.3 s; skills and attributes were picked automatically.

What changed since 1.6.110:

- 1.6.111–1.6.112: the `сверка спавна` (spawn audit) log: every 30 s of battle clock the host logs "created" and "sent to guest", the guest logs "received", "copies created", "gone at the host before a copy"; by type and by kind (normal, special, epic, transition). An enemy id at the guest is counted once per battle.
- 1.6.113: a copy that failed to be created is retried after 2 s (previously never).
- 1.6.114: clock coroutine log: every creation of `Play_Time` with the mod's stack, and a line when two run at once.
- 1.6.115: normal copies take a preform from the normal pool; real boss preforms stay for bosses (see docs/ENEMY_SYNC.md).
- 1.6.116: "copies created" at the guest, also by kind.

The host barely creates special, epic and transition mobs by itself, so they were summoned at the host through `coop_debug` at 0:23–0:40:

- transition: `call mgr.Create_UnitEnemy_Transition`;
- epic: `set mgr.ApplyCreateEpicEnemyID 6`, `ApplyCreateEpicEnemyNum 10`, `ApplyCreateEpicEnemyDelay 0.3` (and the same without `Apply`), then `call mgr.StartCoroutine Create_UnitEnemyEpic`;
- special "Super": `set mgr.SuperEnemyCreateNum 8`, `set mgr.SuperEnemyCreateDelay_Num 99` (created 2).

## Logs

- Before the fix (1.6.111–1.6.114): at 5:00 the guest logged `общие враги: тип 41 не создался` (3 times, and in retries); logcat had a `NullReferenceException` in `C_Battle_EnemyUnit.Start_Unit`. The guest's boss pool had 5 preforms without child objects, the host's had 12 with three. A manual `call mgr.Create_UnitEnemyBoss 41 1` at the guest failed the same way.
- The "created / sent to guest" gap is enemies that died at the host before the first send (0.09 s): up to 4% for the weak types 1, 7, 12, 13.
- "Gone at the host before a copy": 7–20% of normal ones. The Pixel 3a creates ~40–50 copies/s, while the host at peak spawns up to ~70 enemies/s; on the guest's screen up to 20 copies per frame waited to be created in dense waves.
- 2 ids of 4801 were missed; the cause wasn't investigated.
- Clock: at the guest `Play_Time` was created only on the special obelisk pickup and after each boss (`из мода: … GiveArtifactChoice ← CatchUpBoss`), no `шагают 2 корутины` lines; the 2:30 and 5:00 boss waves started for both in the same second.
- No `ОШИБКА [coop]` lines.

## Known limitations

- No player, immortality through debug. On the 5:00 boss wave the host's hero still dropped to 0 HP, and shared enemies at the guest turned off for that time (`хост не присылает врагов`), which interfered with the test.
- Special, epic and transition mobs were summoned manually and in small numbers; special "Super" mobs from the game's waves and epic boss waves weren't tested separately.
- In the 01.11 battle (1.6.113) the guest's clock ran in 2 s steps and didn't stop at the 2:30 boss wave (looks like two `Play_Time`); it didn't recur in later battles, the cause wasn't found, and the `часы:` log is kept.
- The guest's copy creation speed shortfall isn't fixed.
