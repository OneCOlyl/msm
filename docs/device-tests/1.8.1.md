Date: 2026-11-07. Claude Code installed the builds, ran `tools/lab.py` and `tools/phones.py` scenarios, called methods
through the `coop_debug` console, and read `modkit.log` on both phones.

| Phone | Android | Resolution | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| Xiaomi 25042PN24C | 16 | 1440×3200 | host |
| Google Pixel 3a | 12 | 1080×2220 | guest |

Build: commit `35417a8` on `main` (both logs show `кооператив 1.8.0-1-g35417a8-dirty… включён`; "dirty" comes from a
`CLAUDE.md` edit, the mod code is the same). The release commit differs from it only in this file and
`.github/release_notes.md`. The room was created and joined through the console, without a VPN. Shared enemies and
shared pause on, area 1 (`phones.py run --area 1`).

New: the friend's combined spells in their place (anchors for projectiles on hero objects), hero statics in the spell
look, the look sent on change, a cast at the hero itself not pulled to an enemy. These were tested; the rest of the
checklist was not run.

Before the fix, `place` showed the host's Arcane Ray rays at the guest 4.9 units from the guest and 19.5 from the host's
ally, and the constellation (projectiles 75–77) 0.1–0.2 from the guest. In this build the rays are 4.9–5.0 from the
ally (the host has the same distance from its hero), the constellation 1.6–1.7 from the ally. Telekinetic Sword, Soul
Blade and Brandish swords are 0.4 from the friend's ally and don't hang in the map centre.

The 8 FAILs in `place`:

- Crazed Spray, Avatar, Mana Spear, Brandish, Supernova (host → guest): `b19` is one stuck Spirit projectile standing at
  the same world point on both phones. The scenario compares its distance to the hero at the caster and to the ally at
  the friend, so it fails. A separate check: plain Spirit is 2.9 at the caster and 3.1 at the friend.
- Avatar (guest → host) `b1`, Genesis `x76`, Astrape `b75`: these projectiles live under a second, and sampling (about
  three times a second) misses them. They passed in the three earlier runs of this branch.

## Known limitations

- Electric Zone combo zones (Fire Ring, Frozen Heart and others) and permanent Plasma rays are not shown at the friend's
  ally. Tsunami flow speed and volcano duration use the receiver's own values. Details in `docs/MAGIC_REPLAY.md`.
- `perf` was not run: the replay costs more only for anchored projectiles (swapping a hero field for the call).
- 3–4 players, VPN, Wi-Fi drop, minimizing the game and turning the mod off were not tested: these parts did not change
  in 1.8.1.
