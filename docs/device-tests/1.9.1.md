## Known limits

- A friend doesn't always see the bullet fragments of Chain Casting and Fission: they are created on a hit, not by a
  cast. The cause wasn't found.
- The constellation explosion (Constellation and its combos) differs between players: the game picks one of three
  looks at random on a hit.
- Electric Zone combo zones and the permanent Plasma rays still aren't shown at the friend's ally.
- The Anywherelan tunnel dropped for 4–12 s during the check (5% ping loss); a friend's magic isn't visible in those
  seconds. `lab.py` repeats such items.
- `mobs`, `chests`, `perf`, 3–4 players, a Wi-Fi drop, minimizing the game and turning the mod off weren't checked:
  those parts didn't change in 1.9.1. Doppelgangers were checked with one friend.
