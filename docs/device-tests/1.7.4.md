of both phones.

## Logs

- No `ОШИБКА [coop]` or `отключён из-за ошибок` lines. The «прошлый запуск игры завершился аварийно» lines come from
  restarting the game with `am force-stop` while installing builds.
- Projectile size on the two phones was compared through hero fields and screenshots; the console can't read the scale
  of live projectiles.

## Known limitations

- On 11-02 at 23:49 (1.7.4), after the host picked up the Terra special obelisk, the guest's artifact choice window
  stayed under the fade (black screen) and held the shared pause for both. Not retried.
- On the Pixel 3a with 440 enemies the mod took about 27 ms per frame on average, about 12 ms of it for shared enemies
  (creating, killing and moving copies). 1.7.4 didn't change this.
- Not tested: VPN, 3–4 players, network loss and minimising the game, turning the mod off, healing, health bars,
  different screens.
