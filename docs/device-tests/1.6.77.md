`Unit_SpecialVarA=1`, `UnitCreateID=3`, but some of the host's transition mobs were still "normal" (created 1) at the
moment of `Start_Unit`; fixed in 1.6.66.
The ModSim test "guest arena is where the host's is" fails in about half of the runs even without these changes (it
measures by the clock).

Found by the user in a battle on 1.6.66: both picked up the mob power-up, "there were no strong mobs". Per the log the mod
forwarded everything (host: 15 own + 15 from the guest, guest: 31 special copies), but the guest's transition mobs were
created at the host with a single `Start_Unit(type, 1, 0)`; the game sets the strength (health bonus 10000, resistances,
speed ×0.65, size, outline) after it in a coroutine. Fixed in 1.6.67: the guest holds their transition mob for 0.3 s,
reads its fields and sends them to the host (the `sf` array).
With the "all magic at max" cheat, transition mobs (≈10,000 health) die at both within a few seconds; on screenshots 7 s
later they are already gone.

## 1.6.73–1.6.75: highlight (marker and outline) on the guest's transition mobs

The user: "upgraded mobs have no highlight". Look log (1.6.73, after 1.5 s): the host's own transition has `Obj_MonsterIcon`,
sprites `MonsterIcon1/2`; the guest's transition at the host has «icon obj нет» (no icon obj), no sprites. Copies and mobs
from the guest are created by `Create_UnitEnemyBoss` from the boss pool; preforms of the boss pool and the normal pool have
no marker (1.6.74: took a normal pool preform, still no marker). The game's transition takes preforms from
`List_BattleUnitEnemySpecial_Empty` (80 → 66 on the host's transition).

1.6.75: a special enemy (copy at the guest, the guest's transition at the host) is created from a special pool preform
(epic from the epic pool): it is put at the head of the boss pool, and `Create_UnitEnemyBoss` takes it
(«взята (в начале пула боссов)» at both). Check: the guest's transition at the host has «icon obj active True/True»,
sprites `MonsterIcon1/2`. Special pool after the battle: host 80, guest 74 (dead ones return to their pool).

## 1.6.76–1.6.77: highlight of copies at the guest (mob power-up sphere)

The user: "when the sphere that spawns special mobs is picked up, they aren't displayed". Look log (1.6.76): the copy at the
guest has the marker, but `MonsterIcon_Size 0`, `Obj_UnitSprite_Back` and its `Sprite_UnitEnemySpecial_Back` are off; the
host's own transition has 0.71 and they're on. On screenshots the host has mobs with a horned marker and pink outline, the
guest has dark ones without a highlight.

1.6.77: the host sends the marker size and "outline on" (elements 14–15 of the enemy record), the guest sets the size and
turns on the outline (the host does the same for the guest's transition). Check: the copy at the guest has
`MonsterIcon_Size 0.714`, marker and outline on; the guest's screenshot shows the horned marker above the copy.

## 1.6.86–1.6.87: marker and outline of copies, position of the guest's transition

The user: "the host picks up the sphere, and at the guest they look normal". Comparing live objects (28.10.2026): at the
guest's copy the marker has `Transform_MonsterIcon.localPosition (0, 0, 0)`, inside the mob's body, at the host
`(0, 0.85, -5)`; the outline at the host is drawn with the `GUI/Text Shader` shader (fills the silhouette with a color), at
the copy with `Sprites/Default`, so pink on a black image comes out black.
1.6.86: the copy gets the marker position `(0, MonsterIcon_PosY, -5)` and the outline gets `GUI/Text Shader` (`Shader.Find`).

Along the way: the guest's transition mobs appeared at the host around (320, 320), because the guest read its mob's
position after `HideAway` (1.6.84). 1.6.87: the position is read before `HideAway`.

Check of 1.6.87 (transition summoned via the console): the host's transition shows up at the guest as mobs in the same
places, with a horned marker and pink outline; the guest's transition appears at both next to the heroes, with marker and
outline.
