- Load (battle summary): host `кадр за бой (1669 кадров), среднее мс: отправка 0.71, …, союзники и магия 0.25; максимум мс: …, союзники и магия 53.8`; guest `кадр за бой (4307 кадров), среднее мс: отправка 0.35, общие враги 0.55, …, союзники и магия 0.22; максимум мс: …, союзники и магия 25.6`. The maximum of the "allies and magic" part is in the frame where the ally appears (its markers and bar copy are created); after that the markers cost less than 0.1 ms per frame.
- Errors `ОШИБКА [coop]`, `ошибки обработчиков за бой`, `отключён из-за ошибок`: none at either host or guest.

## Known limitations

- The mod takes the arrow sprite from the location selection screen. If the mod is turned on right in battle or the sprite gets unloaded, a drawn ">" arrow is used (not seen on the phones).
- The bar under the ally is green, like the own hero's; the friend's shield (the yellow part of the native bar) isn't sent, so the ally's is empty.
- Two phones and one VPN were tested; connection loss, turning the mod off mid-battle and 3–4 players weren't tested on phones in this release.
