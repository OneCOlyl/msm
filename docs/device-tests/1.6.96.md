# Testing 1.6.96 on phones

Date: 2026-10-30. Tested by: Claude Code over adb (installing the build, debug console, `modkit.log` of both phones), without a player.

| Phone | Android | Resolution | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| Xiaomi 25042PN24C | 16 | 1440×3200 | host |
| Google Pixel 3a | 12 | 1080×2220 | guest |

Connection: `call <session>.Host` at the host and `.Join` at the guest through `coop_debug`.

Version on the phones: `кооператив 1.6.96 включён`: host at 15:05:17, guest at 15:05:35.

Battle mode: shared enemies on (`world=1`, `sendHz=30`), shared pause on. Both phones had `coop_debug` and `coop_cheat` enabled. Heroes immortal: `skip C_Battle_AllyUnit Unit_Death 0`. Skills were picked automatically (the first card), the battle lasted about 9 minutes.

What changed since 1.6.88: shared enemies load at the guest (order of creating and sending copies, off-screen repositioning, kill queue, pool reading) and direct reading of the copies' `Unit_IsLive`/`Unit_Hp` by field offset instead of reflection.
