# Testing 1.6.98 on phones

Date: 2026-10-30. Tested by: Claude Code over adb (installing the build, debug console, `modkit.log` of both phones), without a player.

| Phone | Android | Resolution | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| Xiaomi 25042PN24C | 16 | 1440×3200 | host |
| Google Pixel 3a | 12 | 1080×2220 | guest |

Connection: `call <session>.Host` at the host and `.Join` at the guest through `coop_debug`.

Version on the phones: `кооператив 1.6.98 включён`: host at 17:00:05, guest at 17:00:32.

Battle mode: Terra zone, shared enemies on (`world=1`, `sendHz=30`), shared pause on. Both phones had `coop_debug` and `coop_cheat` enabled. Heroes immortal: `skip C_Battle_AllyUnit Unit_Death 0`. Skills were picked automatically (the first card), measured over 5 minutes of battle.

What changed since 1.6.97: an enemy copy at the guest is killed with `Unit_Hp = 0` by field offset and `Unit_Death()`. Previously it was `SetMember(Unit_Hp, 1)` and `ICombat.Damage(e, 1e9)` with a full `Unit_Damage`. A snapshot of live enemies by address is no longer built for kills.

Before the build: a solo guest battle (Arrays) and a check through `coop_debug` during the skill choice pause: 30 calls of `Unit_Death` (with `Unit_Hp 0`) gave +30 `db.Game_KillMonsterNum` and +30 `enemyDeath` events (the mod's `_kills` counter); 30 empty commands over the same time gave +0.

- Killing a copy: 4,109 ms per battle for 10,358 kills, about 0.40 ms per copy. On 1.6.97 the estimate is the same (about 0.39 ms; there's no exact kill count for that measurement). `Unit_Death` itself is expensive: `StopAllCoroutines`, a string `StartCoroutine` and mods' enemy death interceptors.
- Creating a copy at the guest: 0.91–1.12 ms (pool 0.21–0.23, `Create_UnitEnemyBoss` 0.53–0.71, setup 0.15–0.18). Heavier than in the 1.6.97 measurement (0.65–0.89): fewer world steps per minute (830–1,070 vs 1,100–1,700), a heavier battle; copy creation didn't change in this release.
- No battle summary (`кадр за бой`): nobody left the battle.
- No `ОШИБКА [coop]`, `ошибки обработчиков за бой` or `отключён из-за ошибок` lines at either host or guest.

## Known limitations

- Tested without a player, on two phones, without VPN scenarios, boss waves or boss gathering.
- Mana drops from killed copies weren't checked separately; the game's kill counter and the `enemyDeath` event were checked.
