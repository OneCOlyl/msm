## Logs

- Load (battle summary on 1.6.64). Host: `кадр за бой (14497 кадров), среднее мс: отправка 0.99, Армагеддон 0.02, общие враги 0.02, часы и журнал 0.04, союзники и магия 0.56`. Guest: `кадр за бой (10419 кадров), среднее мс: сеть и пауза 0.01, отправка 1.12, Армагеддон 0.04, общие враги 1.82, часы и журнал 0.12, союзники и магия 1.12`. The guest's maximums are in the first frames of the battle (`создание копии мс: макс 429.1`).
- Per-frame movement at battle start: host 58 fps, guest 24 fps (35 on 1.6.62). The spread between battles on the Pixel 3a is large. Part of the load is copies of all the host's enemies, which the guest keeps even far from the host (up to 127).
- No `ОШИБКА [coop]`, `ошибки обработчиков за бой` or `отключён из-за ошибок` lines at either host or guest.
- Why the guest's spawning stopped after a boss wave (1.6.60, console): with `ApplyBossNum 1`, which the mod holds so the game doesn't give the guest its own obelisk, plus the wave's leftovers, the guest kept `OnlyBossON 1` and `Create_UnitEnemy_Stop` ran every frame. At the host after the boss dies: `ApplyMonsterClearKillNum 0`, `BossWave_SpeedRatio/SizeRatio 0`, `HitDamageRatio 1`, `OnlyBossON 0`. Setting the same values at the guest stopped the halts. After `Create_UnitEnemy_Start` enemies came.

## Known limitations

- On 1.6.64, at the moment of the move the host hadn't yet sent the center of its arena (`арена — вокруг меня`): the guest's move and the start of the host's arena are driven by the same wave-start frame. The mod repeats the arena move every 0.5 s, so a fraction of a second later the guest's arena matches the host's. On 1.6.63, where the move happened after 1.5 s, the log showed `арена — как у хоста` right away.
- The guest's enemies far from the host exist only at the guest: the host doesn't see them, and the host's magic doesn't hit them.
- During a boss wave a guest far from the host has no own enemies (with boss gathering off, the guest stands alone).
- Two phones and one VPN were tested; 3–4 players, connection loss and turning the mod off mid-battle weren't tested in this release.
