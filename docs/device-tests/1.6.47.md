## Logs

- Obelisk at the guest (shared enemies): `обелиск: счётчик боссов гостя держу 1` from the start of the battle; at 3:00 and 6:00, `обелиск: хост поднял свой — у меня босс-волна снята`. The game didn't give the guest its own obelisk (no `игра положила его гостю` and no pickup of item "18").
- Guest freeze after the host's obelisk pickup (pulling ~480 items): 3:00, 0.5 s; 6:00, 2 s (in 1.6.46: 1.2 s and 7 s, then a Mono crash `mono-threads-posix.c:175`, SIGSEGV).
- Load: the slowest frames are in the first seconds of the battle (guest: 533 ms on entry, 305 ms creating the first enemy copies); the battle summary (`кадр за бой …`) wasn't available yet at the time of writing, as the battle hadn't ended.
- Errors `ОШИБКА [coop]`: none at either host or guest.

## Known limitations

- Two phones and one VPN were tested; connection loss, turning the mod off mid-battle, 3–4 players and healing weren't tested on phones in this release.
- The Mono crash in 1.6.46 is random (under load): 1.6.47 reduces the load, one battle up to 6:00 went without a crash, which is no guarantee.
- The room on the phones was created through the debug console on top of one already created at game launch: the guest once got «Сначала войдите в комнату.» (Join a room first.) and reconnected by itself (16:12:55–16:12:57). Normal button taps didn't cause this.
