## Logs

- Found and fixed along the way: on 1.6.117–1.6.118 an area screen opened at the guest after the host changed the area showed the old picture; calling `AreaLevelInf` with the wrong area number drew Terra (only area 14 has difficulty levels).
- No `ОШИБКА [coop]` or `отключён из-за ошибок` lines. The «прошлый запуск игры завершился аварийно» lines come from restarting the game with `am force-stop` while installing builds.

## Known limitations

- Not tested: Terra difficulty, paid area entry at the guest, a guest who hasn't unlocked the host's area, a guest reaching the area screen after the host started.
- Turning shared enemies on once when updating from 1.6 is tested only in ModSim: the phones already had them on.
