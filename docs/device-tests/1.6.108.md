# Testing 1.6.108 on phones

Date: 2026-10-30. Tested by: Claude Code over adb (installing the build, debug console, `modkit.log` of both phones), without a player.

| Phone | Android | Resolution | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| Xiaomi 25042PN24C | 16 | 1440×3200 | host |
| Google Pixel 3a | 12 | 1080×2220 | guest |

Connection: `call <session>.Host` at the host and `.Join` at the guest through `coop_debug`.

Versions on the phones: `кооператив 1.6.105 включён` (host 21:31:11, guest 21:31:31): removal at the guest; `кооператив 1.6.106 включён` (host 22:21:57, guest 22:22:21): pickup by the host and levels; `кооператив 1.6.107 включён` (host 22:54:53, guest 22:52:12): first and second obelisk in one battle up to 3:00. Pickup by the host was also tested on 1.6.104 (21:27).

Battle mode: Terra zone, shared enemies on, shared pause on, `coop_debug` and `coop_cheat` on, heroes immortal (`skip C_Battle_AllyUnit Unit_Death 0`). The host's hero was walked to the obelisk with a joystick swipe.

1.6.108 differs from 1.6.107, which was tested on the phones, by one condition: the branch "the guest's game ended the boss stage by itself" (the guest clears its own obelisk flag and changes the area) fires only if the host has already had a boss wave and the host's obelisk flag arrived as zero. Found through a ModSim failure on CI: the special obelisk flag is set for both at battle start, and if the guest sees it before the host's state arrives, the guest changed the area by itself and lost the reward for the host's pickup. Tested by tests (ModSim 184/184, waiting on the condition); 1.6.108 wasn't tested on a phone.

What changed since 1.6.102: in shared enemies the Terra special obelisk exists only at the host; when the host picks up an obelisk (special or normal), the guest's area changes once, not twice; only the first obelisk before the host's first boss wave counts as special.

How it works in the game (`libil2cpp.so` and the console, 30.10): at battle start on Terra the game gives each player a `C_Battle_Item_TreasureBox` with `ObtainItemType 9` (object name "9") and sets `ApplyObeliskON = 1`. Pickup (`OnCollisionEnter`): `Z_Database.ArtifactType = 9`, `V_Artifact_Manager.Create_Artifact` (the «Obelisk» window), `DestroyItem` (turns off the sprites and collider, the object itself stays active), then the game changes the area. The pickup doesn't pull mana. Before the pickup the battle on Terra doesn't start (clock 0:00, no enemies); for the guest the battle is started by the replay of the host's pickup. `Create_Artifact` itself calls `Start_AreaChange` for `ArtifactType` 9, 10 and 11; an area change on Terra gives a batch of levels (the host goes 1 → 13 right after the pickup, without mana or kills).

## Logs

- Before the fix (1.6.103, the same pickup scheme as for the boss obelisk): on the host's pickup the guest got type-10 artifacts and `притянуто предметов 280`.
- On 1.6.105 the guest had two area changes after the host's pickup (`_Play_AreaChange` twice, `AreaChangeID 1 → 5` vs `1 → 2` at the host): the mod called `Start_AreaChange` itself, and `Create_Artifact` called it again; at 0:06 the guest was level 18 and the host level 13, with 31 and 43 kills. It had been the same on normal obelisks after a boss since 1.6.42.
- On 1.6.104 the obelisk removal at the guest was logged every second of the first minute (wrappers compared by reference); in 1.6.105 by object address, once.
- No `ОШИБКА [coop]` or `отключён из-за ошибок` lines.

## Known limitations

- The game's pickup branch has one more action for one of the `Z_Database` bonuses (`CreateItem_Character(-2, 10)` and a hero shot); it isn't repeated for the guest.
- The special obelisk is told apart from the boss obelisk by the host's clock (there are no boss obelisks before 3:00).
- The area number (`AreaChangeID`, weather and field look) at the guest is picked by the guest's own game and may differ from the host's.
- In the 1.6.107 battle the guest's clock went ~9 s ahead after the special obelisk (0:51 vs 0:42) and stayed so until the boss: the guest hit its own boss wave at 2:31 while the host was at 2:22; after the host's obelisk pickup at 2:31 the guest was set to 2:58. The host caught up by speeding up: the gap went 27 s → 3 s by 3:15.
- The "30 s before the boss" window in clock catch-up assumes a boss every 3 minutes; on Terra it's every 2:30.
- Tested without a player, on two phones.
