## Known limitations

- A chest for the guest's choice becomes shared through the host: with an older mod version at the host such a chest
  is lost. All players need the same version.
- ModKit itself cuts the «Требует: modkit_combat …» line in its card; the mod can't fix it.
- `perf`, 3–4 players, VPN, Wi-Fi drop, minimizing the game and turning the mod off weren't checked: these parts didn't
  change in 1.9.0.
