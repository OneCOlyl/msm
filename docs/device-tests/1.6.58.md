## Logs

- Load (battle summary on 1.6.58): host `кадр за бой (26534 кадров), среднее мс: отправка 1.87, Армагеддон 0.02, общие враги 0.04, часы и журнал 0.05, союзники и магия 0.51; максимум мс: сеть и пауза 4.3, отправка 28.2, Армагеддон 18.2, общие враги 24.6, часы и журнал 20.4, союзники и магия 100.5`; guest `кадр за бой (15441 кадров), среднее мс: сеть и пауза 0.01, отправка 2.47, Армагеддон 0.04, общие враги 3.11, часы и журнал 0.18, союзники и магия 1.16; максимум мс: сеть и пауза 35.7, отправка 252.3, Армагеддон 47.2, общие враги 442.7, часы и журнал 410.6, союзники и магия 374.0`. The guest's maximums are the first frames of the battle (`медленный кадр 737.7 мс` at 16:47:50, creating the first enemy copies `438.0`).
- Errors `ОШИБКА [coop]`, `ошибки обработчиков за бой`, `отключён из-за ошибок`: none at either host or guest.
- The new client queue limits didn't trigger on the phones: no `связь зависла` and no `игра не успевает разбирать входящие сообщения`. In battle (1.6.55, console, every 5 s) the send and receive queues at both held 0–1 messages.
- The guest's crash on 1.6.56 (16:09:00, 2 s after the 3:00 boss wave started): logcat `Assertion at mono-threads-posix.c:175, condition 'info->suspend_source == MONO_THREAD_SUSPEND_SOURCE_RUNTIME' not met`, then `signal 11`. A background thread created after game launch crashed (probably the network one). In 1.6.57 message receiving was reworked without locks between the network and main threads; on 1.6.57 and 1.6.58 battles up to 6:00+ with two boss waves went without a crash.

## Known limitations

- The cause of the 1.6.56 crash isn't proven: the same Mono runtime crash happened on 25.10 on 1.6.46. After the receiving rework it didn't recur in two battles, which is too few to consider it fixed.
- Queue limits (send and receive overflow, reconnection) are tested only by PC autotests: overflow couldn't be triggered on the phones. The `coop_server.py` changes aren't involved on the phones, only in autotests (`tests/server_test.py`, NetTest against the Python server).
- Shared pause now holds as long as a friend is in the choice window or the pause menu and connected, with no time limit: if the friend walked away from the phone in the pause menu, the others wait until they come back or leave the battle.
- Two phones and one VPN were tested; 3–4 players, connection loss at the host and turning the mod off mid-battle weren't tested in this release.
